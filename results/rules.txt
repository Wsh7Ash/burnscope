Google SRE workbook (google-sre)
Four severity-tiered rules: two fast page-worthy burns and two slower ticket-worthy burns, each gated on a long and a short window agreeing.
Reference period: 30d

A rule fires only when BOTH its long and its short window reach the burn-rate threshold.

fast-burn-page  [page]
  long window   1h  (threshold 14.4x)
  short window  5m  (must also reach 14.4x)
  budget used   2.00% of the 30d budget by the end of the long window
  for a 30d period this rule is designed to burn 2.00%
  action        Page the on-call: at this rate 2% of the budget is gone in one hour.

medium-burn-page  [page]
  long window   6h  (threshold 6x)
  short window  30m  (must also reach 6x)
  budget used   5.00% of the 30d budget by the end of the long window
  for a 30d period this rule is designed to burn 5.00%
  action        Page the on-call: at this rate 5% of the budget is gone in six hours.

slow-burn-ticket  [ticket]
  long window   1d  (threshold 3x)
  short window  2h  (must also reach 3x)
  budget used   10.00% of the 30d budget by the end of the long window
  for a 30d period this rule is designed to burn 10.00%
  action        Open a ticket: at this rate 10% of the budget is gone in one day.

long-burn-ticket  [ticket]
  long window   3d  (threshold 1x)
  short window  6h  (must also reach 1x)
  budget used   10.00% of the 30d budget by the end of the long window
  for a 30d period this rule is designed to burn 10.00%
  action        Open a ticket: the budget is being consumed faster than it replenishes.
